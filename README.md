# EVMath

Your home for all EV mathing.

## About

EVMath helps you determine if switching to an electric vehicle is right for you.

### Features

- **EV vs Gas Cost Comparison**: Compare the total cost of ownership between electric and gas vehicles
- **Charging Station Calculator**: Calculate charging costs and time at different stations
- **Personalized Analysis**: Find out if an EV makes financial sense for your specific situation
